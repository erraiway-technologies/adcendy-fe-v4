Buyers have decided on a powder and are choosing which one. They compare format and dietary fit. Black Edition sits directly in this purchase frame.

Readers of roundups checking protein-per-pound and brand credibility. Editorial and creator content shape the shortlist.

Buyers want a complete, convenient meal, not a supplement. Close to Black Edition’s proposition, in different language.
